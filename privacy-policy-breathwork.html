Privacy Policy - Feniks Breath

Last Updated: December 2025

Introduction

Feniks Labs ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how the Feniks Breath application ("the Application") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Data Controller

Feniks Labs

Email: contact@fenikslabs.app

Data Collected

Locally Stored Data

The Application stores only the following data exclusively on your device:

  • Breath Sessions: Start date and time, session duration, completion status, breathing pattern used
  • Application Settings: Preferred breathing patterns, display preferences

This data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or to third parties.

Data Not Collected

The Application does not collect:

  • Personally identifiable information (name, address, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Banking or payment information
  • Biometric data
  • Contacts or contact lists
  • Photos or multimedia files
  • Browsing history or web navigation data

Use of Data

Locally stored data is used solely to:

  • Display your Breath session information
  • Allow management of your breathing sessions
  • Personalize your experience within the Application

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to third parties. All your data remains stored locally on your device.

Permissions

The Application requests the following permissions:

Android

  • Notification Permission (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY): Used solely to enable "Do Not Disturb" mode during your Breath sessions, to provide you with an uninterrupted experience.

iOS

The Application may request access to notifications to allow you to manage interruptions during Breath sessions.

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and is protected by the standard security mechanisms of your operating system. We do not have access to your data and cannot retrieve or modify it.

Your Rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your data: You can view all your data directly in the Application
  • Delete your data: You can delete individual sessions or all your data through the Application's features
  • Uninstall the Application: Uninstalling the Application deletes all locally stored data

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as the Application is installed on your device. If you uninstall the Application, all your data is automatically deleted.

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised update date. We encourage you to review this page regularly.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following services that may have their own privacy policies:

  • App Store / Google Play: For downloading and managing reviews (via native store APIs)
  • Google Fonts: For font rendering (local loading)

Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, please contact us at:

Email: contact@fenikslabs.app

Consent

By using the Application, you consent to this privacy policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Application.
